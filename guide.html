How to play

You're the shepherd's dog. Wolves come in waves and try to grab sheep; the dog scares them off just by getting close. Keep as many sheep alive as you can until the end of summer.

A wave

  1. New sheep join the flock (and any animals you bought).
  2. Wolves arrive from the tree line, prowl for a few seconds, then go for a sheep. From wave 2 they come in pairs from opposite sides.
  3. A wolf inside the dog's reach (the faint ring around it) gets scared and runs. A grabbed sheep is let go if the dog gets there in time.
  4. The shepherd moves the flock to fresh grass every 20-35 s, so the dog has to keep repositioning.
  5. When the timer runs out, the surviving sheep are sheared for wool, and you spend it in the shop.

The goal: End of Summer

Economy

There are two numbers: ★ score, which is only for bragging, and 🧶 wool, which is what you spend. Wool only comes from the flock.
